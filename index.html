How to Play

  • Four players choose one kingdom each. The host starts when all four have joined.
  • Each round lasts 180 seconds. Plan moves, attacks, recruitment and upgrades, then finish your turn.
  • You begin with 6 Action Points (AP). Town Hall upgrade increases this to 8.
  • Movement is horizontal or vertical. Units cannot pass through units, buildings, trees or water.
  • Capture the four Control Points for 3 gold per point each round. Collect gold deposits by moving onto them.
  • Destroy all enemy Town Halls. The last surviving kingdom wins.
